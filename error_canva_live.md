In Canva Whiteboards, there isn't a simple "enable follow" toggle in the settings. Instead, the ability to be followed is usually always on.

If users cannot follow you (the owner) but can follow each other, it is likely due to one of two things: either you need to use the **"Everyone Follow Me"** feature to force the view, or you have a **"Ghost Session"** active that is confusing the system.

Here is how to fix this and enable the feature for your users.

### Solution 1: Use the "Everyone Follow Me" Feature
As the owner/facilitator, you have a special feature that forces all participants to follow your view instantly. This is more reliable than asking them to click your avatar.

1.  Open your Canva Whiteboard.
2.  Click on **your own profile icon/avatar** in the top right corner (next to the Share button).
3.  Select **"Everyone follow me"** from the dropdown menu.
    * *Note:* This will send an invite to all participants. If they don't respond within a few seconds, their screen will automatically snap to your view.

### Solution 2: Fix the "Unclickable Avatar" Issue
If you want users to be able to click your avatar to follow you passively (without you forcing it), and it isn't working, it is almost always because **you have the whiteboard open in multiple tabs or devices.**

* **The Problem:** If you have the whiteboard open in two tabs (or on a phone and a laptop), your avatar appears twice to other users. One avatar is "live," and the other is a "ghost." If they click the "ghost" avatar (which corresponds to the inactive tab), nothing will happen because that version of you isn't moving.
* **The Fix:** 1.  Close **all** other tabs or windows where this whiteboard is open.
    2.  Refresh your current page.
    3.  Ask your users to refresh their pages.
    4.  They should now see a single, active avatar for you that allows them to "Follow."

### Summary of How "Following" Works
* **To Follow Someone:** Participants click the person's avatar in the top right.
* **To Stop Following:** They click the avatar again or simply drag their canvas to move away.
* **To Lead (Owner Only):** Use the "Everyone follow me" feature described in Solution 1.

**Next Step:** Try closing any extra tabs you have open, then use the **"Everyone follow me"** button. Does that successfully pull everyone to your view?
